Privacy Policy

Coffee Saver (package: app.coffeesaver)
Developer: Dirty Tesla (@Dirtytesla)
Last updated: September 1, 2026

What this app is

Coffee Saver is a simple habit tracker. It helps you keep track of café skips and how much your home coffee machine costs you “on paper” compared to buying coffee out. That’s it — local habit tracking, not a social network.

What data is stored

The app stores data only on your device, including things like:

  • Machine name and price
  • Cost per home coffee
  • Skip / café history
  • App settings

There is no login. Habit data is not sent to a Dirty Tesla server. Dirty Tesla does not operate a cloud backend for your Coffee Saver habit data.

Ads, purchases, and permissions

  • Version 1.0 shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
  • The app does not use location permission.
  • The app may include internet permission because of a WebView. In practice, content such as Google Fonts may load from Google’s servers. That means Google may see your device’s IP address when those resources are fetched.

This policy does not claim advertising IDs are unused forever — it only states that ads are not shown in v1.0. If ads are added later, this page will be updated.

Backups and clearing data

If you have Android / Google backup turned on, device backup may sync app data through Google’s backup service. That is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by a Dirty Tesla server.

You can clear local data with the app’s Start over option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Coffee Saver is not directed at children. It is a general consumer habit tool and is not intended for kids.

Changes to this policy

If the app later adds ads, accounts, cloud sync, or other features that change how data is handled, this privacy policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.
