Privacy Policy

Coffee Saver (package: app.coffeesaver)
Developer: Dirty Tesla (@Dirtytesla)
Last updated: September 1, 2026

Short version

Coffee Saver is a skip counter for paying off a home coffee machine on paper. Version 1 for Google Play has no ads, no advertising ID permission, no login, and keeps your habit data on your device.

What the app stores

On your phone only (local storage / app preferences), Coffee Saver may keep:

  • Machine name and purchase price you enter
  • Typical coffee cost and currency
  • Skip history (amounts, times, quick vs exact)
  • Settings such as theme, haptics, and sound

That data is used only to run the tracker and the optional home-screen widget. There is no Dirty Tesla account and no Dirty Tesla server that receives your skips.

You can wipe first-party data in the app with Settings → Start over, or by uninstalling the app. Export JSON (if you use it) creates a file on your device; it is not uploaded by us.

What we do not do (this build)

  • No ads in version 1 (AdMob SDK and advertising ID permission are not in the release)
  • No in-app purchases
  • No login, email signup, or social accounts
  • No location permission
  • No first-party analytics SDK, crash reporter, or cloud sync

Network access

The Coffee Saver release app does not require network access for normal use. Fonts (Figtree / Fraunces) are self-hosted inside the app. Google Fonts CDNs are not used. The Android package does not declare the INTERNET permission in the release merge. Habit data is not sent to Dirty Tesla servers (there are none for this app).

Android may back up app data to your Google account if device backup is on. That is Google’s backup system, not a Dirty Tesla upload.

Children

Coffee Saver is not directed at children and is not part of Designed for Families.

Changes to this policy

If ads, accounts, cloud sync, or other features that change how data is handled are added later, this privacy policy will be updated before those builds ship, and the Play Data safety form will match.
