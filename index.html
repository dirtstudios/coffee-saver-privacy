Privacy Policy

Coffee Saver (package: app.coffeesaver)
Developer: Dirty Tesla (@Dirtytesla)
Last updated: September 1, 2026

Short version

Coffee Saver is a skip counter for paying off a home coffee machine on paper. Builds with ads (including v1.0.2) show a bottom banner ad via Google AdMob. Your habit data (machine price, skips, settings) stays on your device. There is no Dirty Tesla login and no Dirty Tesla server for habit data. Optional tips open Cash App and/or X Money in a browser; tips do not remove ads.

What the app stores

On your phone only (local storage / app preferences), Coffee Saver may keep:

  • Machine name and purchase price you enter
  • Typical coffee cost and currency
  • Skip history (amounts, times, quick vs exact)
  • Settings such as theme, haptics, and sound

That data is used only to run the tracker and the optional home-screen widget. There is no Dirty Tesla account and no Dirty Tesla server that receives your skips.

You can wipe first-party data in the app with Settings → Start over, or by uninstalling the app. Export JSON (if you use it) creates a file on your device; it is not uploaded by us.

Advertising

Ads-enabled versions of Coffee Saver (including v1.0.2) display a bottom banner advertisement served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • The app may use an advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Google Play services advertising identifiers) so AdMob can serve and measure ads.
  • The Android package declares the INTERNET permission so ads can load. Network access is used for advertising (and for optional tip links that open in a browser).
  • AdMob and Google may collect device and ad-related information under Google’s policies. See Google’s advertising / AdMob privacy disclosures for details on what Google processes.

Habit data described above is not uploaded to Dirty Tesla for ads. We do not sell your skip history.

Optional tips (“Buy me a coffee”)

Optional tip actions may open Cash App and/or X Money in your device browser so you can tip if you want. Tips are optional. Tipping does not remove ads and is not an in-app purchase inside Coffee Saver.

What we do not do

  • No Dirty Tesla login, email signup, or social accounts for habit data
  • No location permission
  • No first-party analytics SDK, crash reporter, or cloud sync of habit data to Dirty Tesla
  • No claim that tips remove ads

Network access

Ads-enabled builds declare the INTERNET permission so Google AdMob can load banner ads. Opening Cash App or X Money for an optional tip also uses the browser and network. Fonts (Figtree / Fraunces) are self-hosted inside the app; Google Fonts CDNs are not used for app UI fonts. Habit data is not sent to Dirty Tesla servers (there are none for this app’s habit tracker).

Android may back up app data to your Google account if device backup is on. That is Google’s backup system, not a Dirty Tesla upload.

Children

Coffee Saver is not directed at children and is not part of Designed for Families.

Changes to this policy

If accounts, cloud sync, or other features that change how data is handled are added later, this privacy policy will be updated before those builds ship, and the Play Data safety form will match.
